# Momentus Elite — Getting Started

Source:
Momentus Support Center

Topic:
Getting Started in Momentus Elite

## Overview

The Getting Started curriculum is designed as a nine-week program, covering one level per week. Each level includes trainings, related readings and knowledge-base electives.

## Prerequisite

Before starting the training, users should log in and become familiar with navigating Momentus Elite.

### Introduction to Elite

This training introduces users to logging in on computer and phone and provides an overview of application features and functionality.

### Using Elite On Your Phone

Momentus Elite can be used on a phone. The calendar is the dedicated mobile page.

---

# Level 1 — Foundations

## Elite Foundations

Two concepts are key to understanding Momentus Elite:

- Event structure
- Event status

Related topics include:

- Event statuses
- Blackouts and internal events
- How events are structured

## Financial Foundations

Momentus Elite can track event financials from estimating revenue during the sales process through revenue realization and payment.

Financial workflow includes:

- Financial information for accounts and events
- Revenue tracking
- Financial statuses
- Accounts receivable workflow

---

# Level 2 — Inventory and Pricing

Momentus Elite allows venues to maintain an inventory library for events.

Inventory can include:

- Food and beverage
- Furniture
- Equipment
- Labor

The system can track inventory stock levels and pricing.

## Packages

Packages can bundle goods and services, define pricing and allocate the bundle to events.

# Level 3 — Booking and Calendar

## Booking Events

This area covers adding events to Momentus Elite.

Topics include:

- Event statuses
- Blackouts
- Internal events
- Additional dates
- Spaces
- Series
- Moving an event from initial booking to confirmed

## Calendar

The calendar is central to the Elite account.

Calendar functionality includes:

- Custom views
- Venue defaults
- Privacy settings
- Filters
- Display options
- Mobile viewing

## Space Usage

Space usage tracks the specific purpose for which a booked space is being used.

Examples include:

- Load in
- Event
- Dark
- Load out

## Contact Roles and Staff Assignments

Momentus Elite can track personnel associated with an event.

Examples include:

- Sales manager
- Event coordinator
- Manager on duty
- Billing contact
- On-site contact
- Signatory
